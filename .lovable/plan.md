# Ahmed Ali Portfolio

## Goal
Build a complete multi-page portfolio for Ahmed Ali that presents real product-design work with an editorial, senior-level visual identity and careful scope language.

## Pages
- **Home:** editorial introduction, availability, featured work, design-system preview, concise services/process, and contact call-to-action.
- **Work:** all six projects in a varied case-study index with clear role, platform, contribution, and scope.
- **Project case studies:** six dedicated project pages using a consistent narrative structure, with honest limitations for Ipsilon and NHG.
- **About:** profile narrative, visual experience timeline, strengths, skills, and tools.
- **Services:** six service areas plus the six-step design process.
- **Insights:** editorial article index and four original article pages.
- **Contact:** contact details with placeholders preserved and a polished client-side enquiry form.

## Visual Direction
- Soft off-white canvas, charcoal typography, and a refined vermilion accent.
- Editorial grotesk typography with oversized but controlled headings, compact labels, fine rules, and generous spacing.
- Mostly square or lightly rounded surfaces; restrained shadows and no glass effects, decorative blobs, or stock imagery.
- Original interface compositions for projects and the design-system showcase, built from structured product UI fragments rather than generic illustrations.
- Floating sticky navigation on larger screens and an accessible menu on mobile.

## Interactions
- Subtle page and scroll reveals that respect reduced-motion settings.
- Tactile link and button states, project-image depth shifts, and a restrained pointer-following accent on capable devices.
- Working navigation, case-study links, article links, and form validation with a transparent placeholder-email state.

## Technical Details
- Use TanStack Router files for every shareable page and dynamic project/article detail routes.
- Centralize portfolio data and reusable site components to keep wording and presentation consistent.
- Define all visual tokens in the global Tailwind v4 design system and use semantic color utilities throughout.
- Add unique metadata to every content route and keep the site fully responsive from 375px through 1440px.
- Verify compilation, browser rendering, navigation, mobile layout, menu behavior, and form feedback.

## Content Guardrails
- Do not invent metrics, employers, dates, clients, outcomes, awards, testimonials, contact details, or ownership claims.
- Keep `[ADD EMAIL]` and `[ADD LINKEDIN]` visibly marked until real details are supplied.
- Describe Ipsilon as an approximately 50% interface redesign and NHG as selected screen/feature redesign work.
